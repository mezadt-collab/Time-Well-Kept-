Chapter One

The Stories We Wear

We don't just collect watches. We collect the moments they remind us of.

I never set out to become a watch collector. Like many enthusiasts, it began with a single watch that meant more than the time it displayed. One watch became two. Two became five. Before I knew it, every new addition marked another chapter of my life.

Some celebrate milestones. Some were thoughtful gifts from family. Others remind me of places, friendships, unexpected discoveries, and moments I never want to forget.

Over time, I realised I wasn't collecting watches. I was collecting memories.

Every scratch tells a story. Every worn strap speaks of years spent on the wrist.

Even the watches that receive little wrist time today remain important because they represent who I was when they entered my life.

My collection has no strict theme. Mechanical watches sit beside quartz pieces. Affordable watches share the same space as milestone purchases. Fashion watches remain because of the people who gave them to me. Vintage discoveries live alongside modern tool watches.

There is no single watch that defines this collection. Every piece has earned its place for a different reason. Some commemorate milestones, some were gifts from the people I love, some were bought simply because they made me smile, and others were unexpected discoveries that somehow found their way onto my wrist. Together, they tell a story that no single watch ever could.

This is my story, told through watches.

Every watch tells time.
Mine tell my life.

— TANVEER MEZAD
